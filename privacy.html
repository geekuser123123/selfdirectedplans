Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information Self Directed 401k Dallas collects through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

At a glance

  • We collect only what you send through our inquiry form: your name, email, optional phone number, topic, and message.
  • We use it to respond to your inquiry. We do not sell it or use it for targeted advertising.
  • We do not currently use analytics or advertising cookies.
  • Please never send Social Security numbers, account numbers, or financial documents through the site.
  • Submitting the form does not sign you up for text messages.
  • You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your information at any time.

This summary is for convenience. The full text below controls.

01Who We Are and Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how [Legal business name], doing business as Self Directed 401k Dallas (“we,” “us,” or “our”), collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information through this website and the communications that follow an inquiry.

It applies to information collected through this website. Information you provide during a separate service engagement may also be governed by the written agreement for that engagement.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider automatically processes standard technical data when you visit, such as IP address, browser type, device information, pages requested, and timestamps. This is used to deliver and secure the website.

We do not currently use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or social media pixels. The site stores one small preference in your browser (whether you paused the background video). It is not used to identify or track you. If we add analytics or similar tools, we will update this policy first.

Information we ask you not to send

Please do not submit Social Security numbers, account numbers, passwords, tax returns, or other financial documents through this website. Our form is designed to reject messages that appear to contain a Social Security number. If documents are needed during a service engagement, we will tell you how to provide them securely.

03How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide the information or services you request
  • Schedule conversations and send follow-up communications about your inquiry
  • Operate, maintain, secure, and improve the website, including preventing spam and abuse
  • Keep records of communications and consents
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use

04Communications and Text Messages

If you submit an inquiry, we may contact you by email or phone about that inquiry, as you consented to in the form. You can ask us to stop at any time by replying to an email or contacting us using the details below.

Text messages (SMS): Submitting the inquiry form does not sign you up for text messages. We will only send marketing or recurring text messages with your separate, express written consent. Consent is never a condition of any purchase. If we offer text messages, you can reply STOP to opt out at any time, and message and data rates may apply.

Mobile phone numbers and text messaging opt-in data will not be sold or shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

  • Service providers who help us run the website and manage inquiries, under obligations to protect it. These include website hosting ([e.g., Netlify]), workflow automation ([e.g., n8n]), and our customer relationship management and email tools ([list providers]).

  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, sale, or reorganization of our business, subject to this policy.
